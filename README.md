## What is an OKX SEPA deposit?

A SEPA deposit is a euro bank transfer from your own European bank account to your OKX account.

SEPA stands for the Single Euro Payments Area. It allows participating banks to send euro payments using standardized bank-transfer details, usually including an IBAN and account-holder information.

On OKX, the process works like this:

1. You open the EUR deposit page in your OKX account.
2. OKX displays the bank details assigned to your account.
3. You copy the provided IBAN and any required reference information.
4. You send EUR from a bank account held in your own name.
5. OKX matches the incoming payment to your account and credits your EUR balance.

The important part is that the bank account name must match the verified name on your OKX account. Transfers from friends, family members, or third-party payment providers may be rejected and returned. OKX specifically warns that deposits sent through third-party sources, including services such as PayPal, Wise, or Airwallex, are not supported for this payment route.

## SEPA Standard vs. SEPA Instant

The two main options serve different use cases.

| Deposit method | Typical timing | Availability | Best suited for |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| SEPA Standard | Usually 1–2 business days | Depends on bank processing hours, weekends, and holidays | Normal deposits when speed is not critical |
| SEPA Instant | Often within seconds | Only when your bank and the payment route support instant payments | Funding an account quickly before a trade |

SEPA Instant is not automatically available just because your bank participates in SEPA. Your bank must support instant euro transfers, and OKX must display the option for your account.

OKX says SEPA Instant deposits can be completed in seconds and are available around the clock. The help page also mentions a €100,000 limit for the instant route, but the limit shown inside your account is the one to follow because limits may depend on region, account status, payment method, and verification level.

Standard SEPA transfers are slower because they may be processed during banking hours. A transfer made late on Friday, during a weekend, or on a bank holiday may not begin processing until the next business day.

## OKX deposit limits

There is no single universal SEPA limit that applies to every OKX customer.

The limit shown in your account can depend on:

- The deposit method.
- Your country or region.
- Your identity-verification level.
- Your account history.
- Local compliance requirements.
- The bank-transfer rail being used.

OKX describes several types of limits:

- **Minimum deposit:** the smallest amount accepted in one transaction.
- **Daily limit:** the maximum amount allowed within a rolling 24-hour period.
- **Weekly limit:** the maximum amount allowed during seven consecutive days.
- **Monthly limit:** the maximum amount allowed during 30 consecutive days.
- **Maximum number of deposits:** the number of transfers allowed during a defined period.

These limits may reset on a rolling basis rather than at midnight. For example, a daily limit may become available again 24 hours after a previous transaction instead of resetting at the beginning of the calendar day.

To check your actual limit, open the EUR deposit page and select the SEPA method. The amount displayed there is more useful than a general number found in an old review or forum post.

## What happens if the deposit is delayed?

Most standard SEPA deposits should appear within 1–2 business days, but delays can happen.

Check these items first:

1. Confirm that the transfer was sent to the exact IBAN shown by OKX.
2. Confirm that the sending bank account is registered in your own name.
3. Check whether the transfer was submitted during a weekend or bank holiday.
4. Review whether your bank placed the payment under an additional compliance check.
5. Check your OKX transaction history.
6. Confirm that the reference or payment information was entered correctly.
7. Keep the bank payment confirmation.

If the deposit has not arrived after two business days, OKX recommends checking the IBAN and transaction history and contacting the OKX Assistant with payment confirmation from your bank. The confirmation should show the receiving account details used for the transfer.

Do not send another transfer immediately just because the first one is delayed. That can turn a timing problem into a reconciliation problem involving multiple payments.

## Why was my SEPA deposit rejected or refunded?

The most common rejection reason is a mismatch between the sending bank account and the name on the OKX account.

For example, a transfer may be rejected if:

- A spouse sends money to your OKX account.
- A business account sends funds to a personal OKX account.
- A friend makes the transfer on your behalf.
- The bank account holder name differs from your verified OKX name.
- The payment provider does not confirm the transaction.
- Incorrect bank details were used.
- The transfer cannot be automatically matched.

OKX says rejected deposits are returned to the original sending account. A refund may take up to five business days, depending on the bank and payment route.

The safest setup is therefore straightforward: use a personal bank account in the same legal name as your verified OKX account, copy the deposit details directly from OKX, and retain the transfer receipt.

## Does an OKX SEPA deposit become available immediately for trading?

1. Deposit EUR.

A completed bank transfer does not necessarily mean every subsequent action is instantly available. The account page will show the relevant restriction if a hold applies.
